Daily Anchors
Three non-negotiable moments that structure your day. Everything else fits around these.
Today's Reset — if you've done nothing yet
1.Put your phone in another room for 30 minutes.
2.Open one task. Not all of them. One.
3.Set the timer below for 25 minutes and just start.
4.After it ends, check this box and log it as a win.
Daily Schedule
Rough time blocks — not a prison, just a shape for the day.
8:00 AM
Anchor 1 — Morning Start
Wake, no phone, move your body
5–10 min. Walk around the block, stretch, anything. No phone for first 20 min. This tells your brain the day has started.
ANCHOR · NON-NEGOTIABLE
9:00 AM
Work Block 1 — Priority task
Acting, Writing, or Foundation
Pick ONE thing from your task list. Actors Access submissions, one page of Hula Hoop, one Joy+Us email. Use the Focus Timer. 25–50 min.
FLEX · ROTATE LANES
10:30 AM
Work Block 2 — Second task
Different lane than Block 1
If Block 1 was Acting, do Writing or Foundation here. Keeps momentum across all lanes without burning out on one.
FLEX
12:00 PM
Protected — Lunch + decompress
Actual break. Not phone-doom. Eat.
Step away from the screen. This isn't wasted time — it's what makes the afternoon work possible.
PROTECT
1:30 PM
Work Block 3 — Admin / Comms
Email, submissions, logistics
Actors Access, Joy+Us emails, LiteHouse follow-ups, Presley Clardy logistics, anything that needs a response. Batch it here instead of all day.
FLEX
3:00 PM
Protected — Workout or Walk
Planet Fitness or 30-min walk
3–4x week at Planet Fitness. Off days: walk. This is non-negotiable for your mental state — not just your body. Movement is the reset button.
PROTECT
5:00 PM
Creative Block — Writing or Reel
Hula Hoop pages / Halvsies / Untapped
Evening is when the internal critic quiets. Use it for generative work — dictate scenes, write long-form, edit footage.
FLEX
9:00 PM
Anchor 2 — Wind Down
Phone away. Review tomorrow.
Spend 5 min looking at tomorrow's tasks. Pick ONE thing to start with. Then put the phone in another room. This is how you stop the loop.
ANCHOR · NON-NEGOTIABLE
10:30 PM
Anchor 3 — Actual sleep
Lights out. Not just lying there.
Your body and your brain are your instruments. They need sleep to work. This one is worth protecting harder than anything else.
ANCHOR · NON-NEGOTIABLE
